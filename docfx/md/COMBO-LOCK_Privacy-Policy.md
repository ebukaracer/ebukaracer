# Privacy Policy for Combo Lock Puzzle Game

**Effective Date:** 28-03-26

Thank you for playing **Combo Lock** (“the Game”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the Game.

## Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

### a. Personal Information

We do not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name or email unless you choose to contact us.

### b. Non-Personal Information

We may collect non-personal data automatically, including:

- Device type and model
- Operating system version
- Country or general location
- App usage statistics

### c. Advertising Data

The Game uses third-party advertising services, including **LevelPlay** mediation, which may collect:

- Advertising ID (e.g., Google Advertising ID)
- IP address
- Device identifiers
- Ad interaction data

These are used to provide relevant ads and improve ad performance.

These third-party partners may collect and process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. This may include personalized or non-personalized advertising depending on your settings and region.

You can learn more about how these partners handle your data by reviewing their respective privacy policies.
